Server rules

Short version: play fair, build freely, treat people well. Long version below.

01

No griefing or stealing

Don't break, take, or edit builds and chests that aren't yours — even if unclaimed and unprotected.

02

No hacked clients or X-ray

Fabric client-side mods are allowed for QoL (minimap, zoom, etc), but combat, movement, and X-ray mods are bannable.

03

Keep chat respectful

No hate speech, harassment, or spam. Disagreements happen — keep them civil.

04

Build appropriately

No offensive builds or structures placed to block others' builds, spawns, or paths.

05

One account per player

Alt accounts used to bypass bans, vote-farm, or duplicate claims will be banned alongside the main.

06

Report, don't retaliate

Found a bug, exploit, or rule-breaker? Open a ticket in Discord instead of taking matters into your own hands.
